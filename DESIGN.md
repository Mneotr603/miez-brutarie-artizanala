# Miez design direction
Dark bakery atmosphere derived from the approved Roastwell reference. Product photography, large literary serif headlines, generous cream content surfaces, sparing orange actions and tan rules.
Tokens: background #12100D; accent #EB5E28; tan #B28858; cream #EAE0C8; alabaster #EFECE6; sienna #9E3314. Orange buttons use near-black text for contrast.
Freight and Brandon Grotesque local font slots. Until supplied, Georgia and Trebuchet MS provide explicit fallbacks; these are not represented as licensed production fonts. Russian needs verified Cyrillic coverage.
Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96px. Fluid section margins and 1240px content width. Minimal rounding, restrained borders, no decorative shadows.
Single GSAP entrance and continuous review transport, stopped by reduced motion, pause control, focus, hover and background document state.
